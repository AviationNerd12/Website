Case No. 01
1090 MHz 4th-Order Chebyshev Microstrip Hairpin Bandpass Filter
Designed a 4th-order ($N=4$) Chebyshev bandpass filter centered at 1090 MHz with 45 MHz passband bandwidth ($FBW = 4.13\%$) for ADS-B signal reception[cite: 1]. Derived low-pass prototype $g_i$ values, admittance inverters ($J_{01}/Y_0 = 0.2419$, $J_{12}/Y_0 = 0.0448$), and even/odd mode impedances ($Z_{0e}, Z_{0o}$)[cite: 1]. Folded $\lambda/2$ resonators into U-shaped hairpins to cut board area by $>50\%$ and integrated direct $50\ \Omega$ tapped feeds ($L_{\text{tap}}$) to guarantee return loss $S_{11} < -15\text{ dB}$[cite: 1].
Substrate: FR-4 ($\epsilon_r = 4.4$, $H = 1.524\text{ mm}$, $\tan\delta = 0.015$)[cite: 1] • Rejection: $\ge 30\text{ dB}$ at 1170 MHz[cite: 1] • Trace Spacing: $S \ge 8.38\text{ mil}$[cite: 1]

Case No. 02
Switched-Mode AC-to-DC Voltage Amplifying Boost Converter
Constructed a complete AC-to-DC power converter converting $\pm 7.5\text{ VAC}$ ($10\text{ V}_{\text{peak}}$, 60 Hz) into an intermediate $+10.0\text{ VDC}$ unregulated rail via a 1N4007 full-wave bridge rectifier and bulk capacitive filter ($V_{\text{ripple}} < 1.0\text{ V}_{\text{p-p}}$)[cite: 1]. Integrated an inductive Continuous Conduction Mode (CCM) boost stage ($V_{\text{out}} = V_{\text{in}}/(1-D)$) driven by a 10–100 kHz clock, stepped up from $+10.0\text{ VDC}$ to $+20.0\text{ VDC}$ using single-potentiometer duty-cycle control with output ripple under $100\text{ mV}_{\text{p-p}}$ at 1.0 mA[cite: 1].
Topology: Inductive Boost + Full-Bridge AC-DC[cite: 1] • Range: $+10.0\text{ V}$ to $+20.0\text{ V}$ regulated[cite: 1] • Load: $0.0 - 1.0\text{ mA}$[cite: 1]

Dossier EE-331-A • Semiconductor Characterization & Limiters
Semiconductor Physics, Non-Linear Wave-Shaping & Dynamic Profiling
Empirical bench extraction of diode forward $I\text{-}V$ conduction dynamics, minority-carrier reverse leakage ($I_{\text{leak}} = V_{R1}/1.0\text{ M}\Omega$), and ideality factors ($\eta$) across five current decades ($1\ \mu\text{A}$ to $10\text{ mA}$) for 1N4007, 1N4148, and 1N5819 devices[cite: 1].
  • Wave-Shaping Limiters: Built anti-parallel ($\pm 0.7\text{ V}$), dual-series ($\pm 1.4\text{ V}$), asymmetric Zener ($-0.7\text{ V}$ to $+4.7\text{ V}$), and back-to-back Zener ($\pm 5.4\text{ V}$) clippers[cite: 1].
  • Oscilloscope VTC: Mapped dynamic Voltage Transfer Characteristics in X-Y acquisition mode and extracted dynamic resistance ($R_z = \Delta V_Z / \Delta I_Z$)[cite: 1].
  • Simulation: Verified breakdown transitions and forward turn-on profiles via LTspice .dc sweeps[cite: 1].

Dossier EE-331-B • AC-DC Conversion & Regulation
Bridge Rectification, Capacitive Smoothing & Zener Regulation
Designed and analyzed half-wave and full-wave bridge rectifier circuits driven by $10\text{ V}_{\text{peak}}$ AC transformer lines, calculating exponential discharge decays ($V(t) = (V_p - V_{\text{on}})e^{-t/RC}$) and conduction intervals[cite: 1].
  • Ripple Optimization: Measured fundamental ripple doubling ($2f_{\text{in}} = 120\text{ Hz}$) in full-bridge rectifiers, reducing ripple voltage by $\approx 50\%$ over half-wave stages[cite: 1].
  • Shunt Zener Regulator: Characterized line and load regulation with a 1N4732 ($4.7\text{ V}$) Zener under static and dynamic loading ($470\ \Omega$ to $10\text{ k}\Omega$)[cite: 1].
  • Dropout Modeling: Analyzed failure boundaries when load current forces Zener current below knee current ($I_Z < I_{ZK}$)[cite: 1].

Dossier ECE-233-A • Circuit Theory & Amplifiers
Transient RC Dynamics, Op-Amp Slew Rates & Active Integrators
Hardware verification of first-order passive and active analog systems under time-domain pulse excitation and wideband frequency sweeps[cite: 1].
