1090 MHz 4th-Order Chebyshev Microstrip Hairpin Filter

Ongoing

Synthesized and simulated a 4th-order Chebyshev bandpass filter centered at 1090 MHz with a 45 MHz passband ($FBW = 4.13\%$) for ADS-B signal reception. Derived prototype $g_i$ values, admittance inverters ($J_{01}/Y_0 = 0.2419$, $J_{12}/Y_0 = 0.0448$), and even/odd mode impedances ($Z_{0e}, Z_{0o}$). Folded $\lambda/2$ resonators into U-shaped hairpins to cut board area by $>50\%$ and implemented direct $50\ \Omega$ tapped-line feeds to achieve return loss $S_{11} < -15\text{ dB}$.

Specifications: FR-4 Substrate ($\epsilon_r = 4.4$, $H = 1.524\text{ mm}$, $\tan\delta = 0.015$) | Stopband Rejection $\ge 30\text{ dB}$ at 1170 MHz | Trace Gap $S \ge 8.38\text{ mil}$

AC-to-DC Power Supply & Step-Up Boost Converter

Completed

Constructed a two-stage power supply converting $\pm 7.5\text{ VAC}$ ($10\text{ V}_{\text{peak}}$, 60 Hz) into an intermediate $+10.0\text{ VDC}$ rail via a 1N4007 full-wave bridge rectifier and capacitive filter[cite: 7]. Stepped up voltage from $+9.25\text{ VDC}$ to $+18.65\text{ VDC}$ using an inductive Continuous Conduction Mode (CCM) boost topology ($V_{\text{out}} = V_{\text{in}} / (1 - D)$) driven by a 555-timer clock, with potentiometer duty-cycle control and output ripple verified at $\approx 71.6\text{ mV}_{\text{p-p}}$[cite: 7].

Verified Bench Data: $+9.25\text{ V}$ to $+18.65\text{ V}$ DC Tuning Range | $65.0 - 71.6\text{ mV}$ Ripple (Spec: $<100\text{ mV}$) | CCM Inductive Switching[cite: 7]

3-Channel Analog Audio Mixing Console & Multi-Band Equalizer

Completed

Engineered an analog audio processing console handling two line-level inputs and one electret condenser microphone channel[cite: 5]. Includes an active low-noise preamplifier with DC blocking ($1\ \mu\text{F}$) and high-frequency noise attenuation, feeding into a 3-channel virtual-ground inverting summing amplifier[cite: 5]. A unity-gain buffer isolates the mixer from a parallel active 1st-order Bass, Midrange, and Treble filter bank before driving a low-impedance speaker output stage[cite: 5].

Specifications: $\pm 15\text{ V}$ Rails | Active Op-Amp Equalizer (250 Hz, 1 kHz, 4 kHz center frequencies) | Dual-Channel DSO Verification[cite: 5]

UW EE 331

Semiconductor Physics & Non-Linear Wave-Shaping

Characterized forward conduction and minority-carrier reverse leakage across silicon rectifiers (1N4007), fast-switching diodes (1N4148), and Schottky diodes (1N5819) over five decades of current ($1\ \mu\text{A}$ to $10\text{ mA}$). Extracted ideality factors ($\eta$) and mapped dynamic Voltage Transfer Characteristics (VTC) in oscilloscope X-Y mode.

  • Built symmetric ($\pm 0.7\text{ V}$, $\pm 1.4\text{ V}$) and asymmetric Zener overvoltage limiters.
  • Extracted dynamic Zener breakdown resistance ($R_z$) and forward resistance ($R_f$) in LTspice.
UW EE 331

AC-DC Rectification, Smoothing & Zener Regulation

Evaluated half-wave and full-wave bridge rectifier circuits driven by $10\text{ V}_{\text{peak}}$ AC transformer inputs. Quantified exponential filter discharge dynamics ($V(t) = (V_p - V_{\text{on}})e^{-t/RC}$) and ripple doubling effects ($2f_{\text{in}} = 120\text{ Hz}$).

  • Benchmarked line and load regulation with a 1N4732 ($4.7\text{ V}$) Zener diode stage.
  • Modeled regulator drop-out thresholds where load pulls current below knee current ($I_Z < I_{ZK}$).
UW ECE 233

Transient RC Response & Op-Amp Bandwidth

Measured transient step responses of first-order RC networks on digital storage oscilloscopes, validating rise time ($t_r \approx 2.197RC$) and propagation delay ($t_{pd} \approx 0.693RC$) against theoretical derivations.

  • Measured large-signal slew rate limits ($SR = |dV/dt|_{\max}$) across $-10\text{ V}$ to $+10\text{ V}$ transitions.
  • Constructed shunt-stabilized integrators ($R_2 \parallel C_f$) to prevent DC offset rail saturation ($\pm 12\text{ V}$).
